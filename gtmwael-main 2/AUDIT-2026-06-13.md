## Positives confirmed (no action)

- **Published article bodies are clean.** The 17-file match for hallucination patterns was the legitimate `author: "Wael Aouididi"` byline. Zero residual `20% of active` or `Landing Page Playbook 2026` body citations — the manual Unbounce fixes held, and no other article carries invented metrics from this pattern set.
- **Judge is genuinely V5.** Rewrite was completed in a prior session. The remaining need for `--force` is real content quality (score <90), not a broken rubric.
- **SSR structure is healthy, not thin.** Gong dist: `h1=1, h2=7, h3=17, jsonld=1`. Stripe: `h1=1, h2=11, h3=15, jsonld=1`. The HANDOFF "thin H2 structure (only 2 content H2s)" worry is contradicted — React components (CtaEvolutionTable, LessonCards, BusinessContextBlock) emit real headings into the SSR HTML.
- **Layer orchestration resolves.** `compose-all-sections.ts` runs Layer 3 → 4 → 1.5 → research → 3.5 → sections → lesson cards → cross-section → judge, each with skip flags and non-fatal guards.
- **Cost is controlled.** ~$0.85 (stripe) to $1.56 (unbounce, rerun-heavy) per article.
