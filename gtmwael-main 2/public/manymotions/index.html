Positioning

One promise, said everywhere.

Every axis below repeats the same message, so it comes first. It is built from five parts, filled in with what your best customers say.

  1. Best customers: the clients who buy fast, spend most and stay longest. Your billing data already holds them.
  2. The change: what is now possible that was not before, and why it matters to them today.
  3. The villain: the thing they want to leave behind. A thing, not a feeling. Your own site already points at the brief and the shoot.
  4. The promised land: the state they reach with ManyMotions, not a list of features.
  5. The simple promise: one sentence a client can repeat to a colleague from memory.
Working direction, to be confirmed with your customers:Lead with the finished ad, not the clip, and with the tool that works inside Claude and ChatGPT.

The scale plan

Six axes to grow on.

Proof comes first, because every other axis uses it. The rest build on channels you already have or can enter early.

Axis 1

Proof from your clients

You have about 1,000 clients. Turn the ones who measure their ad results into case studies: the result first, then how they made ads before, why they chose ManyMotions, how they use it, and what comes next.

Axis 2

Inside AI assistants

The MCP server, the Claude connector, the ChatGPT apps and the API are already live. Write each app and MCP description like a landing page, add one case study of a client who makes ads from Claude or ChatGPT, and measure this channel apart from the website.

Axis 3

Search and content

Own one topic: how to make a product ad with AI. Your blog already leans that way, and the 101+ templates can each anchor a group of pages. Once the English pages show which ones convert, copy them into the languages the product already speaks. Each output becomes a clip, a blog section and a carousel.

Axis 4

Your own audience

Grow the email list from trials and signups first. Then open an Instagram account and post what the product makes. Track list growth, direct traffic, branded search and return visitors.

Axis 5

Agencies

A pilot on a short list of 25 to 100 agencies that look like your best Agency clients, run over 90 days. Personal outreach by email and LinkedIn, one owner per account, and the goals set before launch. One opener: make their client's ad from a product link, live, on the first call.

Axis 6

Community

Start with 25 to 50 hand-picked members from your most active clients. Give them a recurring challenge as a reason to meet, and thank the early contributors in public.

Ideas to think about

Four ways to get more ads made.

Affiliate program

Clients and creators earn a share of the revenue they bring. The commission and how long it runs depend on your margin on credits.

Creator sponsorship

Sponsor smaller creators whose audience matches your best customers: sellers, marketers, agencies. Each sponsored post shows a video made on ManyMotions.

The 100 creators test

Hire 100 freelancers, for example on Upwork. Each one gets free credits and $100 to make one concept. Before launch, decide what counts as a win (views, shares or signups) and give all 100 the same brief. The winners become the formats you scale.

The contest

Same budget, other shape: one $10,000 prize at the end. Anyone can enter with a video made on ManyMotions, so each entry is a new person trying the product. You get public reach and one winner, with less control than the 100 creators test.

Higgsfield already runs this play. Its Talent Contest offers $1,000,000 in cash prizes across 20 awards, and every AI image and video in an entry must be made on Higgsfield. It also runs an “Adathon”, with $50,000 for first place.

Order of work

Who first, then the rest.

  1. Find your best customers in the data and talk to them.
  2. Write the positioning and the promise from what they say.
  3. Put it in their words on the site and test the copy.
  4. Publish the first case studies.
  5. Build the AI ads pages, then add languages.
  6. Grow the audience: email list, Instagram, community, creators.
  7. Take a baseline before the first campaign, then measure every quarter.

The agency pilot and the assistant channel can start as soon as the promise is written, since both use channels that already exist.

Next steps for your team

Talk to 6 to 10 clients.

One conversation each, not a survey. The goal is their exact sentences, which become your copy.

Before the calls, pull from your billing and product data which plan your longest-retained clients are on, which product they use most, and what business they are in. Then ask:

  1. What did you type into Google before you found ManyMotions?
  2. What did you use for your ads before, and why did you switch?
  3. What made you sign up when you did?
  4. How do you describe the problem to a client or a partner?
  5. What would you lose if ManyMotions disappeared?
