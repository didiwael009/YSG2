# Blog publishing rules (readability)

Adopted by Wael on 2026-11-11. Read before writing or editing any article on /blog.

These rules come from measuring 13 Medium articles that read easily against our three benchmark articles. Only 4 of the 13 are proven hits (Pueyo, Tristan Harris, Karpathy "Software 2.0", "How it feels to learn JavaScript in 2016"); the other 9 are recent free articles. Treat the numbers as targets, not laws.

## The 8 rules

1. **Paragraphs.** Median of 25 words or fewer. At most about 1 paragraph in 10 runs past 60 words. Mix in one-sentence paragraphs, about 1 in 3, without turning the whole page into one-liners.
2. **Opening.** The first 2 sentences give my own stake, in the first person, or the surprising number. Not the method, not the brief.
3. **Finding early.** The verdict sits in the title, the subtitle or the first third of the article. The method comes after.
4. **Section headings state a claim** ("The winner writes half the filler"), not a label ("Round 1").
5. **Images.** One image, chart or screenshot every 150 to 300 words. No long stretch of text alone.
6. **Title.** 10 to 15 words, with a stake or a number.
7. **Ending.** Finish on an action or a question. Sources fold under the conclusion.
8. **Tables.** 1 or 2 per article at most, only where a comparison needs one.

## The measurements behind them

Medians. "Ours" = blog / video / animation benchmark, as measured on 2026-11-11 before the rewrite.

|---|---|---|
| Words per paragraph | 25 | 39 / 48 / 11.5 |
| Paragraphs over 60 words | 9% | 17% / 32% / 0% |
| One-sentence paragraphs | 38% | 22% / 27% / 96% |
| Words per heading | 215 | 161 / 130 / 33 |
| Words per image | 271 | no image / 124 / 37 |
| Tables | 0 | 6 / 2 / 0 |
| Title words | 11 | 15 / 10 / 4 |
