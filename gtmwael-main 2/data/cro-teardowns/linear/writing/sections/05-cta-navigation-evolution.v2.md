## Linear's CTA evolution: from waitlist to dual-funnel conversion

### What changed

Linear replaced a single call to action — **"Request Early Access"** — with two parallel paths: **"Get started"** (self-serve) and **"Contact sales"** (enterprise). 

The navigation also shifted. Linear removed **"About us"** and **"We're hiring!"**, then added **"Contact"**, **"Log in"**, **"Open app"**, and **"My issues"** — the last two targeting existing users, not first-time visitors.

### Who the dual-CTA structure filters out

The new structure assumes visitors already know whether they want self-serve or sales. People still learning what Linear does — or whether it fits their workflow — no longer have a middle option. 

The old waitlist was low-commitment: join without starting a trial or booking a call. That path is gone. Both CTAs now require a decision the page does not help visitors make.

### Audit your own page

Open your homepage and count CTAs above the fold. Do you offer both a no-commitment path (like Linear's old waitlist) and a direct trial? Or only high-commitment actions?

If all CTAs require trial signup, demo booking, or sales contact, visitors still researching may leave. This structure may work if your brand is known enough that traffic arrives pre-segmented — but most early-stage products do not have that luxury.
