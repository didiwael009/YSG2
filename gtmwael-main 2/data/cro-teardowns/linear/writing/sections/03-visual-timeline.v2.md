## Linear's homepage positioning: the headline no longer names the product category

### What changed

Linear's headline no longer tells you what the product is. The 2020 version opened with **"The issue tracking tool you'll enjoy using"** — a product category statement. The current version reads **"The product development system for teams and agents"** — a job title, not a product type.

The page now assumes you already know Linear is an option. It speaks to the outcome you want, not the tool you're comparing.

### Why it matters

The old headline spoke to someone searching for issue trackers. The new one speaks to someone rethinking how their team builds products. Linear now positions itself beyond the "issue tracking" category — it competes as the way to run product development, not just one step inside it.

If the visitor does not already recognize Linear as a contender before the page loads, this framing may read as abstract. The page no longer explains what Linear *is* in the first sentence.

### What Linear's homepage positioning shift costs in clarity

Test your own headline: open it in a private browser window. Does the first sentence tell a cold visitor what your product does, or does it assume they already know?

Visitors who search "issue tracking tool" may not recognize Linear solves their problem. The page traded category clarity for positioning breadth.
