## Linear's conversion path: from waitlist to dual-funnel CTAs

### What changed

Linear went from a single action — **"Request Early Access"** — to multiple conversion options split by buyer type. The homepage now offers **"Get started"** (self-serve) and **"Contact sales"** (enterprise) as parallel paths. The navigation added **"Contact"** and **"Log in"**, and removed **"About us"** and **"We're hiring!"** The page also added **"Open app"** and **"My issues"**, both shortcuts for existing users rather than first-time visitors.

### Who this filters out

The new structure assumes visitors already know whether they want to try the product themselves or talk to sales. People who are still learning what Linear is — or whether it fits their team at all — no longer have a low-commitment option like "learn more" or "see how it works." The old waitlist let anyone join without committing to a trial or a sales conversation. That path is gone.

### Audit your own page

Open your homepage and count how many CTAs appear above the fold. Then ask: does each one serve a different decision state, or are they all pushing the same action with different words? Linear's dual-funnel structure works because the product is well-known enough that visitors arrive already segmented. If your traffic is still mixed — some ready to buy, some still learning — check whether you are offering a path for both.
