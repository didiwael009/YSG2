## Linear's homepage positioning: how the headline changed from "issue tracking" to "product development system"

### What changed

The 2020 headline opened with a product category: **"The issue tracking tool you'll enjoy using"**. The current headline names a broader outcome: **"The product development system for teams and agents"**.

Linear no longer starts by naming what the tool is. It starts by naming what the visitor is trying to accomplish — and it assumes the visitor already knows Linear is one way to do it.

### Why it matters

The old headline spoke to someone comparing issue trackers. The new one speaks to someone comparing ways to run product development.

That distinction signals category expansion — when a company stops competing inside its original product category and positions itself as the solution to a larger problem. The page now assumes the visitor sees Linear as more than bug tracking software.

If your brand does not already own that expanded category in the buyer's mind before the page loads, this framing reads as vague.

### What it costs

Visitors who search "issue tracking tool" may not recognize Linear solves their problem. The page no longer explains what the product *is* in the first line.

Test your own headline: open it in a private browser window. Does the first sentence tell a cold visitor what your product does, or does it assume they already know?
