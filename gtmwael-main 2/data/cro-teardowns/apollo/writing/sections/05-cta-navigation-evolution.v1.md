## How Apollo's conversion path narrowed over seven years

### What changed

Apollo replaced ten CTAs with ten new ones. The early homepage offered granular product feature CTAs: **"Find & Prioritize"**, **"Scoring Engine"**, **"Enrich & Refresh"**, **"A/B Testing"**. The current page consolidates to three action paths: **"Get started for free"**, **"Book a free demo"**, and **"Get a demo"**. The shift moves from educating visitors about what each feature does to filtering for visitors who already know they need sales intelligence software.

### Who this filters out

The old CTAs served people still learning what Apollo could do for them — visitors comparing different types of sales tools or trying to understand whether they need contact data, email sequencing, or analytics. The new CTAs assume the visitor already knows Apollo is a sales engagement platform and is now choosing between self-serve signup and talking to sales.

### Audit your own page

Count how many of your CTAs explain what happens next versus how many assume the visitor already decided. If most of your traffic comes from non-branded search or cold ads, feature-level CTAs may convert better than a single "Get started" button. Apollo can compress the path because their brand does the education work before the page loads.
