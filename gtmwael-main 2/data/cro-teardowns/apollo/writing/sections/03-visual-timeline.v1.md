## Apollo's homepage positioning: how the headline changed from 2019 to 2026

### What changed

The original headline read **"No one ever drowned in revenue"** — a playful opener followed by a full paragraph explaining the product's function. The current headline reads **"The AI sales platform for smarter, faster revenue growth"** — a category statement with no explanation below it. The page no longer walks visitors through what Apollo does. It expects them to arrive already knowing.

### Why it matters

The new homepage assumes the visitor already understands what a sales platform is and what Apollo is known for. This is category leadership — when your brand does the explanation work before someone reaches your site. Apollo can write **"The AI sales platform"** because most visitors already searched for Apollo by name or clicked through channels where the product was already introduced. The page no longer converts cold traffic. It confirms what warm traffic already believes.

### What it costs

This positioning may cost conversions if a meaningful portion of your visitors still arrive without brand awareness — from non-branded search, cold paid ads, or referral links that do not prequalify intent. To check: filter your analytics to landing page sessions from non-branded organic search. If that segment represents more than 20% of homepage traffic, removing the category explanation introduces friction for visitors who need it most.
