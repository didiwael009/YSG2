## Agorapulse CTA and navigation homepage changes: from third-party review links to on-page conversion

The buttons moved in step with the words.

### CTA changes: what Agorapulse stopped sending visitors away to read

The removed CTAs assumed visitors needed external proof before deciding. **"Read on TrustRadius"**, **"Read on G2 Crowd"**, **"Read on GetApp"**, and **"Reviews on G2CROWD"** all directed visitors off the page to third-party review platforms.

The added CTAs — **"Try for free now"**, **"Watch an On-Demand demo"**, **"Book a demo"**, and **"Sign up!"** — assume the visitor already knows what they want and is ready to act. This suggests the homepage no longer treats third-party review sites as a necessary stop, at least if visitors are arriving with enough familiarity to skip that step.

### Navigation changes: how Agorapulse's homepage nav shifted toward on-page action

**"Sign up!"** was added directly into the top navigation bar — a conversion step visible before a visitor reads any copy. That addition signals the page may now expect visitors to arrive already oriented, rather than needing to browse first.

What was removed supports this. **"Learn more"** and **"Read more"** are gone — both are category-style labels that nudge visitors toward more content rather than a decision. **"Schedule Freely"** was also removed, suggesting the page dropped a feature-specific entry point that may have served a narrower audience.

**Founder test:** Open your own homepage navigation. If your primary nav items still look like **"Learn more"** or **"Schedule Freely"** — labels that point toward content rather than action — your navigation may be orienting visitors instead of moving them toward a decision. Count how many nav items link to an action versus a category page.
