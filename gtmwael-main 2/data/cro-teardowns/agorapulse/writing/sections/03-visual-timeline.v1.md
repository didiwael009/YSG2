## Agorapulse homepage 2019–2025: from playful cloud hero to dark-navy ROI platform

### 2019 — the sky-blue starting point

The 2019 homepage opened with a light blue, cloud-style background, colorful floating social media icons, and a centered headline: **"Social Media Management Simplified."** Everything about the layout — the bright palette, the playful icons, the low-key proof — pointed at a friendly tool for small teams. Navigation included a plain **"Features"** label. The page was explaining what Agorapulse was, not filtering for who should buy it.

### October 2019 to 2020 — the charcoal pivot

By late 2019, the sky blue was gone. The hero shifted to a dark charcoal background with a white headline: **"Managing social media has never been easier."** A product screenshot appeared beside the copy. The palette change alone moved the page from playful to serious — even though the underlying message stayed in explanation mode, focused on features and ease.

The 2020 snapshot kept charcoal but pushed the product screenshot to center stage, making features the visual anchor. Proof was still thin. The tone was growing up; the positioning hadn't caught up yet.

### 2024 to today — split-column, navy, and ROI framing

The 2024 layout introduced something new: a split column with testimonial proof below the fold, and a headline shift to **"Take Control of Your Social Media."** A visual sophistication score of 4 out of 5 — described in the evidence as polished — replaced the earlier 3.

The current page goes further. Dark navy replaces charcoal. The headline now reads: **"Drive Real Impact on Social Media. From Engagement to ROI."** The product UI sits in the right column. The page title dropped "Simple & Affordable" and became **"Social Media Management Software | Agorapulse"** — quieter, more category-confident.

The colour shift from sky blue to navy is not a refresh. It signals a different buyer entirely.
