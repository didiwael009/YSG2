## How Unbounce replaced 'SEE THE PLANS' with product-specific entry points — and what that did to the sales motion

### How Unbounce's CTAs changed — from single funnel to multiple entry points

The original CTAs assumed one type of visitor: someone ready to act fast. **"PREVIEW THE LANDING PAGE BUILDER"** and **"SEE THE PLANS"** both point to a buyer moving toward a decision. The new set assumes something different. **"Start building for free"**, **"Start A/B testing"**, **"Learn about Concierge plans"**, and **"Learn the basics"** each address a different level of intent — from curious beginner to enterprise evaluator.

That spread signals a shift away from pushing every visitor toward a single conversion. The trade-off is real: when no single CTA owns the page, visitors with high intent may not self-identify quickly enough — and leave before converting.

### How Unbounce's navigation changed — from builder preview to product suite

Early navigation pointed visitors directly at the product. The eight items removed — including **"Learn More"** and **"Browse Our Template Gallery"** — suggest the company no longer needs to walk visitors through basics on arrival.

What replaced them signals a broader audience. Eight items were added, including **"Request a demo"** and **"Get support"** — paths that serve enterprise prospects and existing customers alongside new self-serve signups.

The removal of gallery and tutorial-style links suggests Unbounce expects visitors to arrive already knowing what a landing page builder does.

**Founder test:** Count how many CTAs appear above the fold on your homepage. If it is more than two, ask which buyer each one serves — and whether a visitor with genuine purchase intent can find their path in under five seconds.
