## How Unbounce's homepage CTAs shifted — from single funnel to product-specific entry points

### How Unbounce's CTAs changed — from one path to many

The original CTAs assumed a visitor close to a decision. **"PREVIEW THE LANDING PAGE BUILDER"** and **"SEE THE PLANS"** both point toward someone ready to commit or compare pricing.

The new set assumes a wider range of visitors. **"Start building for free"**, **"Start A/B testing"**, **"Learn about Concierge plans"**, and **"Learn the basics"** each address a different stage — from someone just exploring to an enterprise evaluator (a buyer at a large company who needs sign-off from multiple stakeholders before purchasing).

That spread may signal a shift away from pushing every visitor toward one conversion path. The risk — untested here — is that a visitor ready to buy has no obvious fast path when several CTAs compete for equal attention.

### How Unbounce's navigation changed — from product preview to broader audience

Eight navigation items were removed and eight were added across the period studied. The evidence does not list the individual nav items verbatim, so no specific labels are named here.

What the CTA evidence does show is that items like **"Learn More"** and **"Browse Our Template Gallery"** were removed from the CTA set. One interpretation is that Unbounce no longer needed to walk first-time visitors through basics — though that holds only if most visitors were already arriving with some familiarity with landing page tools.

What replaced them — CTAs including **"Request a demo"** and **"Get support"** — suggests the page may now be serving enterprise prospects and existing customers alongside new signups.

**Founder test:** Unbounce currently shows at least four distinct CTAs above the fold. Count yours. If you have more than two, check whether each one maps to a different type of buyer — and whether someone ready to purchase can find their path in under five seconds without reading every option.
