## lemlist homepage positioning: how the headline changed between 2019 and 2026

### What changed

The 2019 headline was **"Send cold emails that get replies"**. By June 2026, it had become **"The AI Outbound Platform for Relevant Outreach at Every Scale"**.

The first version explained what the product does. The second names a category — **"outbound platform"** — and assumes you already know what that means.

### Why it matters

The new headline no longer explains the product. It declares category membership instead. If a visitor is unfamiliar with outbound platforms, the headline provides no definition — they must translate platform language into their own problem first.

### What it costs

lemlist removed the explanatory scaffolding. The original headline stated the outcome directly. The section heading **"Your first campaign in less than 10 minutes"** disappeared entirely. If your traffic includes visitors unfamiliar with your category, this approach assumes knowledge they may not yet have.

Test: open lemlist's current homepage. Count how many words in the H1 explain what the product does (zero) versus what category it belongs to (all ten). If the ratio favors category, you are filtering for category-aware buyers only.
