## lemlist's CTA shift: from instant trial to demo-first conversion

### What changed

Between 2019 and 2026, lemlist replaced **"Start your free trial now"** and **"Start now"** with **"Get a demo"** and **"Book a demo"**. The page removed **"Start your 14 day Free Trial"** entirely. By Jun 2026, **"book a personalized demo"** appears as the primary CTA, while **"Start a 14-day free trial"** moved lower on the page.

### What the new path requires

The dominant path now asks visitors to schedule a meeting before accessing the product. Earlier versions let visitors start immediately — no calendar, no sales conversation required first. The new structure works if the visitor already decided to evaluate lemlist specifically, not if they are still comparing categories or testing tools quickly.

### Audit your own page

Open your homepage. Count the CTAs above the fold. How many grant instant access — trial, freemium, sandbox — versus those requiring a meeting? Compare that ratio to your homepage one year ago. If demo CTAs increased and self-serve CTAs dropped, check whether inbound trial volume changed in the same period.
