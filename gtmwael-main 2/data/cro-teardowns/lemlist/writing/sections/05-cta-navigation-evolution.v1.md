## lemlist's CTA evolution: from self-serve trial to sales-assisted conversion

### What changed

lemlist replaced **"Start your free trial now"** and **"Start now"** with **"Get a demo"** and **"Book a demo"**. The page removed **"Start your 14 day Free Trial"** and added **"Start a 14-day free trial"** at different points — but the dominant shift was toward demo requests. By 2026, **"book a personalized demo"** appears alongside trial CTAs, where earlier versions offered only self-serve signup.

### Who this filters out

People who want to test the tool on their own before talking to sales. Visitors who are comparing tools quickly and expect instant access — not a meeting with a rep. This path assumes the buyer already knows they want a sales engagement platform and is ready to evaluate lemlist specifically, not still deciding whether outbound email is worth doing at all.

### Audit your own page

Check whether your primary CTA requires a meeting or grants immediate access. If you added a demo CTA in the last year, compare your trial-start rate before and after. If it dropped and qualified pipeline did not rise proportionally, the demo gate may be filtering out buyers who convert better through self-serve exploration.
