## lemlist homepage positioning: how the headline changed from 2019 to 2026

### What changed

The original headline was direct: **"Send cold emails that get replies"**. By June 2026, it had been replaced with **"The AI Outbound Platform for Relevant Outreach at Every Scale"**.

The first version told you what the product does. The second version names a software category and assumes you already know what "outbound platform" means.

### Why it matters

The new headline no longer explains the category. It states membership in one — this is category positioning, where the company assumes visitors already understand what an outbound platform is and why they might need one.

That shift narrows the page's effective audience. The visitor who arrives asking "how do I get more replies to cold emails?" now has to translate platform language into their own problem first.

### What it costs

Visitors unfamiliar with the term "outbound platform" may not recognize this page solves their problem. If your homepage still depends on non-branded search or discovery traffic, removing the problem statement may reduce conversions from that segment.

Test: search Google for your own product category. If the first-page results all use different language than your headline does, you may be speaking to the wrong stage of buyer awareness.
