# lemlist Homepage Teardown: How a Cold Email Tool Became an AI Outbound Platform

Between July 2019 and June 2026, lemlist replaced its outcome-focused headline **"Send cold emails that get replies"** with the category-defining claim **"The AI Outbound Platform for Relevant Outreach at Every Scale."** The page stopped explaining how to run a single cold email campaign and started showcasing a multi-channel prospecting toolkit — complete with a lead database, phone finder, and signal-based automation. The navigation no longer points to educational resources like Academy or Product Tour; it now surfaces specific capabilities that enterprise sales teams evaluate during vendor comparison. By the end of this teardown, you will know when this type of platform expansion messaging makes sense for your own homepage — and when it risks confusing the buyers who already convert.
