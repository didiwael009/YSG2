## Hootsuite homepage positioning: how the headline changed between 2023 and 2026

### What changed

The original headline opened with **"Grow your reach and get more business with social media. Let's do this."** — a benefit-first promise aimed at anyone managing social accounts. The current headline reads: **"Drive real business impact with the world's deepest social intelligence and management platform"** — capability language that assumes the visitor already knows what social media management software does and is now comparing platform depth.

### Why it matters

The new headline expects visitors to arrive already familiar with the category. It positions Hootsuite as the most advanced option in a known field, not as an introduction to what social media management software can do. This is category leadership positioning — when a company stops explaining the category and starts claiming first place within it. The page now optimizes for visitors who already know they need this type of tool.

### What it costs

Visitors arriving without category context — founders exploring whether they need a social tool, or marketers unfamiliar with "social intelligence" as a concept — may not understand what the page offers. To check if your homepage depends on category explanation: remove your company name from the headline and show it to someone outside your industry. If they cannot name the product category, your page still needs explanatory messaging.
