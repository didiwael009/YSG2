## Hootsuite homepage positioning: how the headline changed between 2023 and 2026

### What changed

Hootsuite replaced beginner-friendly benefit language with expert capability framing. The original headline opened with **"Grow your reach and get more business with social media. Let's do this."** — a promise aimed at anyone managing social accounts. The current headline reads: **"Drive real business impact with the world's deepest social intelligence and management platform"** — language that assumes the visitor already knows what social media management software does.

### Why it matters

The new headline is written for visitors who already understand the category. This is category leadership positioning — when a company stops explaining what the category is and starts claiming first place within it. The page no longer introduces what social media management software can do.

Instead, it compares platform depth against alternatives the visitor is assumed to already know. If visitors arrive without this context, they may struggle to understand what the page offers.

### What it costs

To check if your homepage depends on category explanation: remove your company name from the headline and show it to someone outside your industry. If they cannot name the product category, your page still needs explanatory messaging. Hootsuite can afford to skip the category explanation because the brand name already carries that context. Most companies cannot make that assumption — their visitors arrive without knowing what the product does, and removing the explanation may cost conversions if non-branded traffic makes up a meaningful share of homepage visits.
