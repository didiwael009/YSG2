## Why Brevo renamed itself and then rewrote its entire homepage

Sendinblue was founded in 2012 and spent a decade building an affordable, multi-channel alternative to Mailchimp for European SMBs. By 2022, it had over 400,000 customers across email, SMS, WhatsApp, and chat. The Brevo rebrand in May 2023 was designed to shed a name that had become associated with a narrow product (email newsletters) and claim a broader category identity: "customer engagement platform." The January 2023 Wayback capture shows the transition — the page title still reads **"Brevo (formerly Sendinblue)"** and a section heading announces **"Sendinblue is now Brevo!"** to visitors arriving with the old brand in mind.

The competitive context explains the urgency. In 2022–2023, Klaviyo went public, positioning itself as the definitive ecommerce email and SMS platform. HubSpot expanded its CRM into email automation and chat. Mailchimp was acquired by Intuit in 2021. Each competitor was staking a specific category claim. Sendinblue/Brevo needed a name and a homepage that could compete across all three — and the old name was too associated with email newsletters to make a CRM or omnichannel claim credible.

The 2026 homepage shows the strategy paying out in measurable ways: customer count grew from 500,000 to 600,000, **"Why Brevo?"** was removed from the nav (meaning visitors no longer need the brand justified), and the H1 now claims the LTV-optimization category rather than brand relationship-building. For SaaS teams considering a rebrand, Brevo's timeline suggests 2–3 years to convert a rebranded homepage from explanation mode to category-ownership mode — and the removal of "Why [Company]?" from the nav is the clearest public signal that the transition is complete.
