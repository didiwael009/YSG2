MailerLite's biggest homepage change between 2015 and 2026 was rewriting its H1 from a single-product email pitch into a five-category platform headline: **"Create email marketing automations landing pages signup forms websites your audience will love."** The page now speaks to independent creators and solopreneurs who already know they need a full marketing suite — not first-time email tool buyers. If your brand name signals simplicity (like "Lite") but your H1 lists five products, you risk confusing visitors who don't already trust you.
