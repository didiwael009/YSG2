## How $450M in funding, a SaaS downturn, and Gartner's new Magic Quadrant forced Gong to outgrow its own category

Gong raised $200M in Series D funding in 2020, then $250M in Series E in 2021, scaling fast on a category it largely invented. By 2022, that same category became a liability. Sales team downsizing across SaaS hit per-seat pricing models hard, and ZoomInfo's acquisition of Chorus consolidated a direct rival while the Clari-Salesloft merger created a broader platform threat.

The homepage shifts are consistent with a company responding to that pressure. The H1 moved from **"REVENUE INTELLIGENCE"** to **"Revenue AI Built To Predict churn"** — from owning a label to proving an outcome. The **"What is RI?"** nav item disappeared entirely. If that removal was intentional rather than incidental, it suggests Gong no longer considered category education a necessary homepage function.

Gartner published its first Magic Quadrant for Revenue Action Orchestration in December 2025, naming a category above conversation intelligence. The page title changed from **"Revenue Intelligence Technology for Sales Teams | Gong.io"** to **"Gong - Revenue AI OS"** — a shift from describing what the product does to naming what it is. If your own page title still contains your category label, check whether your largest customers still describe your product the same way.
