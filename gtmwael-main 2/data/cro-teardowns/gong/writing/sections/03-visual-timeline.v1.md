## Gong homepage 2020–2025: from cinematic category launch to enterprise product OS

### 2020 — launching a category nobody had named yet

The 2020 homepage opened with a full-bleed gradient hero, large illustrated characters on the right, and a single bold statement on the left: **"REVENUE INTELLIGENCE"**. No product screenshots. No customer counts. The navigation even included a link labeled **"What is RI?"** — the page assumed visitors needed the category explained before anything else.

This was a page built to introduce an idea, not evaluate a product.

### The shift — from category education to product credibility

The page title tells the story cleanly. It moved from **"Revenue Intelligence Technology for Sales Teams | Gong.io"** to **"Gong - Revenue AI OS"**. The illustrated characters disappeared. Individual sales testimonials — **"Paul Santarelli VP of Sales"** and **"Bevin Lyon VP, Customer Strategy"** — were removed. The meta description shifted from sales conversation optimization to **"multimodal revenue signal processing, specialized AI agents, and purpose-built applications."**

That is a different reader being addressed entirely.

### 2025–2026 — a product that expects to be evaluated

The current page uses a split layout: headline and CTA on the left, an interactive chat widget mockup on the right, soft gradient background. The headline reads **"Revenue AI Built To Predict churn"**. A section now leads with **"Trusted by 5,000+ customers"** and **"Winning GTM organizations run revenue on Gong."**

The lifestyle illustration is gone. The product interface is front and center.

**Your test:** Visit your homepage and cover the logo. Does the design alone signal what stage of buyer you are speaking to — someone learning what your product is, or someone ready to evaluate it?
