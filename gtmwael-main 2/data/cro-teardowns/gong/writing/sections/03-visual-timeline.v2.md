## Gong homepage 2020–2026: from cinematic category launch to enterprise product OS

### 2020 — launching a category nobody had named yet

The 2020 homepage opened with a full-bleed gradient hero, large illustrated characters on the right, and one bold statement on the left: **"REVENUE INTELLIGENCE"**. No product screenshots. No customer counts. The navigation included a link labeled **"What is RI?"** — the page assumed visitors needed the category explained before anything else could land.

### The shift — from category education to product credibility

The page title moved from **"Revenue Intelligence Technology for Sales Teams | Gong.io"** to **"Gong - Revenue AI OS"**. The meta description shifted from sales conversation optimization to **"multimodal revenue signal processing, specialized AI agents, and purpose-built applications."** Both changes point away from explaining what the category is and toward describing a product buyers are expected to already want.

The illustrated characters disappeared. Individual sales rep testimonials — **"Paul Santarelli VP of Sales"** and **"Bevin Lyon VP, Customer Strategy"** — were removed and replaced with **"Trusted by 5,000+ customers."** Removing named individual reps in favour of a customer count suggests the page is now written for procurement teams evaluating at scale, not individual contributors exploring a new category.

### 2026 — current state

The current page uses a split layout: headline and CTA on the left, an interactive chat widget mockup on the right, soft gradient background. The headline reads **"Revenue AI Built To Predict churn"** (the raw evidence string includes a rendering artefact: *"Revenue AI Built To Predict churnPredict churn"*). A section leads with **"Winning GTM organizations run revenue on Gong."** The visual language is enterprise-grade — product interface front and center, lifestyle illustration gone.

**Your test:** Go to your homepage navigation. Is there a link that explains what your category *is* — the way Gong's 2020 nav included **"What is RI?"** — or does every link assume the visitor already knows what you do? If the former, your page is still in education mode.
