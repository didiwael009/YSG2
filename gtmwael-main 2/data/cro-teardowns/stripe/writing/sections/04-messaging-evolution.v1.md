## Stripe's messaging shift: from payment platform to financial infrastructure

### Who the old page served

The old page spoke to someone trying to understand what Stripe does. The headline said **"Global Payments"** — category labeling, not benefit. The meta description explained that Stripe is **"a suite of APIs powering online payment processing"**. This served visitors who needed to learn what the product category was before they could evaluate whether Stripe was right for them.

### Who the new page serves

The new page speaks to someone who already knows they need payment infrastructure and is now evaluating options. The headline became a positioning statement: **"Financial infrastructure to grow your revenue."** The meta description now says Stripe **"helps all types of businesses accept payments, build flexible billing models, and manage money movement."** This is vendor-comparison vocabulary — someone deciding between Stripe, Adyen, or building in-house.

### What this means for the sales process

The page now expects a sales conversation, not immediate self-serve signup. The language assumes the visitor is comparing platforms and needs to understand breadth of capability before committing. Does your homepage still explain what your product category is — or does it assume visitors already know and are choosing between you and a competitor?
