## Stripe homepage positioning: how the headline changed between 2022 and 2026

### What changed

The 2022 headline read **"Global Payments"** — two words that named the product category. The 2026 headline reads **"Financial infrastructure to grow your revenue. Accept payments, offer financial services, and implement custom revenue models—from your first transaction to your billionth."** The page stopped explaining what Stripe is and started describing what you can build with it.

### Why it matters

The new headline assumes you already know Stripe processes payments. It speaks to buyers evaluating scope and flexibility — custom revenue models, financial services, scale from first transaction to billions. 

This is category leadership positioning: when a company is so well-known it can skip the product explanation and speak directly to outcomes. The page now addresses visitors who are comparing platforms, not visitors still learning what payment infrastructure means.

### What it costs

If your homepage receives traffic from buyers unfamiliar with your category — through cold ads, non-branded search, or referrals — removing the product explanation may send them elsewhere before they understand what you offer. Test this on your own page: does your headline assume the visitor already knows what you sell, like Stripe's does? Or does it still explain the category? If you removed that explanation today, would a cold visitor understand what problem you solve?
