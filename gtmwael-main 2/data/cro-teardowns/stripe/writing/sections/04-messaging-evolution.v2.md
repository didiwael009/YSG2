## Stripe's messaging shift: from explaining payments to comparing capabilities

### Who the old page served

The old page spoke to someone still learning what Stripe does. The headline read **"Global Payments"** — a category label, not a pitch. The meta description said Stripe is **"a suite of APIs powering online payment processing"** — language that defines the product category before making any claim about why you should choose it.

### Who the new page serves

The new page speaks to someone comparing payment platforms. The headline became **"Financial infrastructure to grow your revenue"** — a claim about what Stripe does for you, not what Stripe is. The meta description now says Stripe **"helps all types of businesses accept payments, build flexible billing models, and manage money movement"** — three capabilities listed side by side, the way you write when the reader is deciding between options.

### What Stripe's messaging shift means for your homepage

Stripe moved from explaining the category to listing capabilities. The old headline named the category. The new one assumes you already know what payment infrastructure is and need to understand what Stripe's version includes. 

Open your homepage in a private window. Count how many sentences explain what your product category is. Then count how many compare your capabilities to what else exists. Stripe went from explaining first to comparing first.
