## Stripe homepage positioning: how the headline changed between 2022 and 2026

### What changed

The old headline read **"Global Payments"** — two words that named what Stripe sold. The new headline reads **"Financial infrastructure to grow your revenue. Accept payments, offer financial services, and implement custom revenue models—from your first transaction to your billionth."** The page stopped naming the category and started describing the outcome and scope.

### Why it matters

The new headline assumes you already know Stripe processes payments. It speaks to what you can build on top of that foundation — custom revenue models, financial services, scale from zero to billions. This is category leadership positioning: when a company no longer needs to explain what it does because buyers arrive already knowing. The page now filters for visitors evaluating *how far* the platform can take them, not *what* the platform is.

### What it costs

If a meaningful portion of your homepage traffic arrives without knowing your category, removing the product explanation may send them elsewhere before they understand what you offer. Test this on your own page: open an incognito window, land on your homepage as a cold visitor, and see if the headline alone tells you what the company sells. If it does not, you are not yet in a position to skip that step.
