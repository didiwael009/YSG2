## Stripe's CTA changes: from sales contact to instant signup

### Stripe's CTA path: sales contact removed, self-serve signup added

Stripe replaced its entire CTA path — 10 buttons removed, 10 added. **"Contact Sales"** and **"Request an invite"** disappeared. **"Get started"** and **"Sign up with Google"** replaced them. The old path routed visitors toward sales qualification. The new path routes them to account creation.

### Who the new CTA path may exclude

Visitors who clicked **"Contact Sales"** in Oct 2022 now see **"Get started"** — a self-serve signup that creates an account immediately. That path may not serve buyers who need pricing quotes or implementation planning before they can create an account — often because internal approval processes require cost estimates first.

If your homepage traffic includes buyers from larger companies who expect to speak with a sales rep before signing up, a self-serve-only path may cost qualified conversations.

### Audit your own page

Open your homepage. Find your primary CTA. Count how many clicks separate it from account creation versus a contact form. If account creation is one click away, you match Stripe's new path. If your CTA routes to a sales form first, you match the old one. The question: does your product require implementation planning that buyers need to discuss before they can activate?
