## Stripe's CTA shift: from sales-assisted to self-serve activation

### What changed

Stripe removed **"Contact Sales"** and **"Request an invite"** from the primary conversion path. The homepage added **"Get started"** and **"Sign up with Google"** — both aimed at immediate account creation. The old path routed visitors toward sales qualification or a waitlist. The new path sends them directly into product activation. **"Start with payments"** disappeared. **"Watch now"** and **"Read the guide"** replaced it — lower friction, still forward motion.

### Who this filters out

The new path may not serve visitors who need pricing clarity or implementation estimates before they commit to creating an account. Sales-assisted buyers — often from larger companies with procurement requirements — expect a contact form, not a self-serve signup. If your traffic includes buyers who cannot activate without internal approval, removing the sales path costs qualified pipeline.

### Audit your own page

Open your homepage. Count how many clicks separate a visitor from account creation versus a sales conversation. If self-serve signup appears first and contact sales is buried in the footer, you are optimizing for activation speed. If your product requires implementation planning or custom pricing, that ordering may hurt conversion for your best-fit accounts.
