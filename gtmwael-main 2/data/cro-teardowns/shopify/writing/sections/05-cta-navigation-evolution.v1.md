## From exploration to instant trial — Shopify removed every mid-funnel ask

Ten CTAs became ten different CTAs. The labels shifted from educational entry points — **"Start your business"**, **"Explore more examples"**, **"Explore ways to sell"** — to product-specific action — **"Start for free"**, **"Get a stunning store"**, **"Website Builder"**, **"Shop App"**. The page removed every "learn more" and "explore" variant. What replaced them: feature-specific trial entry points and tool names that assume you already know what Shopify does. The path now serves buyers who've done their research elsewhere. The tradeoff: speed vs qualification — faster for the ready, invisible for the curious.

The new CTA set can be read as a shift from nurturing consideration to capturing intent. Shopify appears to have moved educational load off the homepage and onto content, ads, or organic search. If your homepage traffic is cold — referrals, brand search from vague awareness — removing exploration CTAs may cost you conversions. If it's warm — retargeting, comparison search, word-of-mouth with context — product-specific CTAs let qualified visitors self-select faster. Count your mid-funnel asks, then ask whether your traffic quality justifies removing them.
