Between July 2021 and June 2026, Shopify rewrote its homepage headline from **"The platform commerce is built on"** to **"Be the next AI all-star"** — a shift that moved the frame from infrastructure authority to founder aspiration. The page title, meta description, navigation structure, and section headings all changed in the same window. This teardown walks through what moved, what it signals about Shopify's target buyer, and where the tradeoffs landed.
