## Should you copy Shopify's homepage messaging shift? When it works and when it doesn't

### The pattern

The headline changed from **"The platform commerce is built on"** to **"Be the nextAI all-star"**. The first states the category. The second assumes you already know it. This signals a shift from explaining what Shopify is to filtering for visitors who already understand that — and are ready to see themselves in it.

### Who should copy this

Shopify's navigation changed from **"Start your business"** and **"Business name generator"** to **"Website Builder"** and **"Domains"**. The new labels assume the visitor already knows they need an ecommerce platform and are now comparing specific features. You can make this shift if your brand name already completes the category sentence before the homepage loads.

### Who should NOT copy this

If your homepage traffic comes primarily from non-branded search, cold paid ads, or referrals that do not mention your category, this approach may confuse visitors who land without knowing what you sell. Section headings like **"Bring your business online"** disappeared from Shopify's page — headings that explained the job to be done. If your visitors still need that explanation, removing it creates friction.

### The test before you copy

Open Google Analytics and filter homepage traffic by source. If more than 30% arrives from non-branded search or paid social, your visitors likely do not know what your product does before they land. That means your homepage still needs to state the category, not assume it.
