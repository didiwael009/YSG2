## Shopify homepage messaging shift: from founder-intent to category-ownership language

### Who the old page served

The 2021 page spoke to someone deciding whether to start selling online. The headline — **"The platform commerce is built on"** — positioned Shopify as infrastructure everyone already uses. The meta description promised **"start a business or grow an existing one"**, addressing founders at the beginning of their research.

### Who the new page serves

The 2026 page assumes the visitor already knows what Shopify does. **"Be the next AI all-star"** skips the product category entirely and speaks to ambition instead. The meta description now leads with **"Build or grow your business fast with AI"**, framing Shopify as a tool for people who have already decided to sell online and are now choosing which platform can accelerate execution.

### What this means for the sales process

The new headline no longer explains what Shopify is. That works if most visitors already know the category — through brand search, referrals, or content they read before landing. It does not work if your traffic arrives cold (visitors who arrive without prior knowledge of your product). Open your homepage in incognito and count how many words a first-time visitor reads before they understand your product category. Shopify's 2026 homepage assumes you already know. Does yours?
