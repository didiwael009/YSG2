## How Vercel's conversion path shifted from product education to product portfolio

### What changed

Vercel replaced 10 older CTAs with 10 new ones. The old CTAs explained platform features: **"SSL encryption"**, **"asset compression"**, **"cache invalidation"**, **"global edge network"**. The new CTAs are product names: **"AI Gateway"**, **"Sandbox"**, **"Vercel Agent"**, **"v0"**, **"CI/CD"**, **"Fluid Compute"**. The page stopped teaching what infrastructure means and started listing what Vercel ships.

### Who this filters out

People who are still learning what a frontend platform does. The old page told you why edge networks and SSL matter. The new page assumes you already know — and that you arrived looking for a specific product in Vercel's stack. If you do not know what **"Fluid Compute"** is before you click, the CTA label will not tell you.

### Audit your own page

Open your homepage. Read each CTA as if you had never heard of your product. Does the button label explain what happens next, or does it rely on brand recognition? If your traffic comes from non-branded search or cold ads, every CTA needs to work without prior context.
