## How Vercel's conversion path changed

### What changed

Vercel removed ten CTAs and added ten new ones. The old homepage included **"Contact"**, **"Contact Us"**, and **"Login"** — standard navigation for buyers ready to evaluate or access the product. Those are gone. The new CTAs point to product features: **"AI Cloud"**, **"Vercel Agent"**, **"CI/CD"**, **"Fluid Compute"**. The path no longer funnels visitors toward a single conversion point. It distributes them across feature pages.

### Who this filters out

This removes the path for visitors who are ready to start a trial or talk to sales but need guidance on where to go next. Someone landing on the page cold now has to choose from multiple feature-specific CTAs without a clear "start here" option. The new structure assumes the visitor already knows which part of Vercel's platform they need — or they will browse feature pages until they figure it out.

### Audit your own page

Open your homepage and count how many CTAs compete for attention above the fold. If the answer is more than two, ask: does a first-time visitor know which one to click? Vercel can afford to distribute attention across ten feature CTAs because their brand carries intent. Most companies cannot. If your visitor does not already know what you do, a single clear CTA will convert better than ten feature links.
