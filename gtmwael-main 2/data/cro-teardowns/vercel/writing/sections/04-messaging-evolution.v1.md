## How Vercel's homepage messaging shifted from developer workflow to AI infrastructure

### Who the old page served

The 2021 homepage spoke to frontend developers choosing a deployment platform. The headline **"Develop. Preview. Ship."** named the workflow steps a developer goes through. The meta description promised **"the best frontend developer experience"** — language for someone comparing tools, not someone building an AI product or working at scale.

### Who the new page serves

The 2026 homepage speaks to teams building on AI infrastructure. The headline **"Build and deploy on the AI Cloud"** positions Vercel as infrastructure, not a deployment tool. The meta description now mentions **"frameworks, workflows, and infrastructure"** — broader language that suggests buyers who need more than a simple hosting service, likely teams with backend requirements or AI integration work.

### What this means for the sales process

The page now expects visitors who already know what Vercel does and are evaluating it against other cloud platforms. The shift from "developer experience" to "infrastructure" suggests the company may now rely more on sales conversations for larger deals, rather than purely self-serve signups.
