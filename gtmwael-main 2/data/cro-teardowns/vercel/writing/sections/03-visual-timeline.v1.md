## Vercel homepage positioning: how the headline changed between 2021 and 2026

### What changed

The original headline was **"Develop.Preview.Ship."** — a product workflow statement. The current headline reads **"Build and deploy on the AI Cloud."** The meta description shifted from **"Deploy web projects with the best frontend developer experience and highest end-user performance"** to **"Vercel gives developers the frameworks, workflows, and infrastructure to build a faster, more personalized web."** The page stopped naming its product behavior and started naming the category it owns.

### Why it matters

The new headline assumes the visitor already knows what Vercel does. It skips the product explanation and opens with a category claim — this is category leadership, when the brand name already completes the sentence before the visitor reads the page. That only works when most traffic arrives through branded search, word-of-mouth, or content that pre-educates the buyer. If your homepage still serves cold search traffic, removing the product explanation means those visitors must infer what you do from context alone.

### What it costs

Visitors who land without prior exposure to the brand no longer get a direct product statement in the first five seconds. The tradeoff is clarity for authority. To check if your homepage can afford this: open an incognito tab, search a generic category term your product ranks for, and land on your homepage. If you cannot explain what the product does in one sentence after reading the headline, you still need the product statement. Vercel can skip it. Most companies cannot.
