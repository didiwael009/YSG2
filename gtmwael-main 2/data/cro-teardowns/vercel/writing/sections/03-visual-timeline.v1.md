## Vercel homepage positioning: how the headline changed from 2021 to 2026

### What changed

The headline dropped the product workflow and replaced it with category ownership language. The original read **"Develop.Preview.Ship."** — three words naming what the product does. The current version reads **"Build and deploy on the AI Cloud."** The shift moves from naming the steps in the tool to naming the infrastructure category itself.

### Why it matters

The new headline assumes the visitor already knows what Vercel is and is now deciding whether to use it. That is category leadership — when the homepage no longer explains the category, because the brand name already carries that context before the page loads. The old headline taught you what the workflow was. The new one assumes you recognize "AI Cloud" as a deployment destination.

### What it costs

A visitor who arrives without knowing what Vercel does will now leave with a vaguer sense of the product. The page trades explanation for positioning confidence. To test whether your homepage still needs the category explanation: open it in a private window, read only the headline, then ask someone unfamiliar with your product what the company does. If they cannot answer, you still need the explanation.
