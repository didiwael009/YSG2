## From "support customers" to "the only helpdesk for AI"

Intercom's homepage now asks visitors to believe a new category exists—and that Intercom owns it.

In January 2023, the H1 read **"Support customers at exactly the right moment"**—a capability claim any helpdesk could make. By October 2025, this shifted to **"Intercom is the AI customer service company"**—an identity statement. The current H1 lands harder: **"The only helpdesk designed for the AI Agent era."** That's not a feature pitch. It's a bet that buyers already accept "AI Agent era" as a meaningful frame.

The belief embedded here: legacy helpdesks are architecturally unsuited for what's next. This framing may resonate with buyers who've already decided AI agents matter—but it assumes that decision. Visitors who haven't bought into the AI-native premise might find the claim abstract or premature.

The tradeoff: outcome promise vs category claim. The old headline promised a result anyone could evaluate. The new one requires accepting Intercom's market thesis first.

**So what?** If your market already debates "the AI era" in your category, a category claim can work. If you'd be educating buyers on the premise itself, lead with outcomes instead—category ownership costs you anyone who hasn't arrived at your worldview yet.
