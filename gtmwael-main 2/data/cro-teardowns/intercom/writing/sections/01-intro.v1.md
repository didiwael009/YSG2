Intercom's homepage underwent a complete rewrite between January 2023 and June 2026. The primary headline shifted from "Support customers at exactly the right moment" to "The only helpdesk designed for the AI Agent era." Navigation was overhauled — eight labels removed, five added — and the page added five new section headings while removing one. The meta description dropped social proof language ("over 25,000 global organizations") in favor of a platform differentiation claim and a free trial push.

The repositioning signal is direct: Intercom moved from framing itself around outcome timing to framing itself around AI differentiation and category ownership. The new headline makes a singular claim — "the only helpdesk" — that creates immediate proof burden and changes what the rest of the page must deliver. This teardown walks through the messaging rewrite, navigation restructuring, visual timeline, CTA changes, and the conversion implications SaaS founders can study when repositioning a homepage under competitive pressure.
