## How Intercom's messaging shifted its target audience

### Who the old page served

The January 2023 headline promised to **"Support customers at exactly the right moment"** — language that assumes the visitor already runs customer support and wants better timing or personalization.

The meta description mentioned **"25,000 global organizations"** as social proof and described Intercom as helping with **"personalized conversations and automated support"** — vocabulary that explains what the product does rather than assuming category knowledge.

### Who the new page serves

The new headline **"The only helpdesk designed for the AI Agent era"** uses three category terms — "helpdesk," "AI Agent," and "era" — that require pre-existing knowledge. The old headline used zero.

The meta description leads with **"natively integrated AI Agent"** and ends with **"Start your free trial today"** — language for visitors who are comparing platforms and ready to test, not learning what Intercom is.

### What this means for the sales process

The new headline uses three category terms that require pre-existing knowledge; the old headline used zero. This may work if inbound traffic arrives already comparing helpdesk platforms — but creates friction if visitors land without knowing what Intercom does.

Count how many category-specific terms appear in your headline. Then ask three non-customers if they understand what your product does from the headline alone.
