Intercom's homepage changed most visibly between January 2023 and June 2026 in its primary headline, navigation structure, and section organisation across four snapshots.

The headline was fully rewritten, changing the audience signal — a shift that can alter who self-qualifies to stay on the page and who bounces. When a SaaS company rewrites its H1 without changing the product, the message is repositioning the buyer, not the feature set.

Navigation was consolidated: eight items removed, five added. Fewer nav options can reduce choice paralysis for casual visitors, but the tradeoff is that enterprise buyers scanning for specific capabilities may not find their entry point in the top bar. Navigation pruning often signals a move from portfolio sprawl to a unified platform narrative.

Section headings shifted substantially — five new H2 elements added, one removed — which may indicate the page now leads with different use cases or buyer entry points. The structural change is observable; what it optimises for is not.

Collectively, the evidence shows a homepage that underwent compositional restructuring, not incremental refinement.
