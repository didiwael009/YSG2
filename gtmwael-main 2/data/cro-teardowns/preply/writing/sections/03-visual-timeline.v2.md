## Preply homepage visual timeline: Jan 2021 to Aug 2026

### The starting point: aspirational lifestyle messaging (Jan 2021)

In January 2021, Preply's homepage opened with **"Prepare to speak confidently with the best online tutors"** — a confidence outcome promise, not a speed or efficiency claim. Section headings reinforced the same frame: **"Make the world your comfort zone"** and **"Focus on the skills you need"** addressed visitors still imagining what language learning could do for their lives, not visitors actively comparing tutors. The page was written for someone at the beginning of a decision, not the end of one.

### The shift: aspiration out, efficiency and proof in

The most visible change in the evidence is the headline itself. **"Prepare to speak confidently"** was replaced by **"Learn faster with your best language tutor."** Alongside this, six section headings were removed — including **"Make the world your comfort zone"** and **"100% Satisfaction Guarantee"** — while four new ones appeared: **"Progress starts with the right tutor"**, **"How Preply works:"**, **"Lessons you'll love. Guaranteed."**, and **"Become a tutor"**. The addition of **"Book a demo"** as a visible CTA suggests a second audience emerged: corporate or team buyers, not just individual learners.

### Where it landed: proof-first with a dual audience (Aug 2026)

The current page is more precise about what it offers and who it serves. **"Progress starts with the right tutor"** replaced the lifestyle headlines. Tutor counts became visible: **"Chinese tutors 5,253 teachers"**, **"Arabic tutors 3,651 teachers"** — scale proof where none existed in 2021. Navigation expanded by 8 items with nothing removed. Visit your homepage and count how many section headings describe a feeling versus a measurable outcome — that ratio is the same trade-off Preply appears to have resolved.
