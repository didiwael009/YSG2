Preply's biggest homepage change between January 2021 and August 2026 was its headline rewrite: from **"Prepare to speak confidently with the best online tutors"** to **"Learn faster with your best language tutor."** The new version assumes the visitor already wants a tutor and is choosing between providers, not still deciding whether online learning is for them. If your audience is still learning they have a problem, efficiency messaging will not land.
