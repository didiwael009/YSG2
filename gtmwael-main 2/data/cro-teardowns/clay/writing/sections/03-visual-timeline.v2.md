## Clay homepage positioning: how the headline changed from 2021 to 2026

### What changed

The 2021 homepage opened with a one-word headline: **"Clay"**. The page also included a section heading that explained the core function: **"This spreadsheet fills itself."**

The current homepage opens with **"Go to market with unique data — and the ability to act on it"**. The self-filling spreadsheet line no longer appears.

### Why it matters

The new headline assumes visitors already know what Clay is. This is category positioning — when a company stakes a claim in an established market rather than explaining what the market is.

The page now treats "go to market with unique data" as something the visitor already wants. It no longer explains what data enrichment means or why spreadsheets need to fill themselves.

### What it costs

If your homepage depends on non-branded search or referrals from general startup content, removing the category explanation may reduce conversion among visitors who do not yet know what problem you solve.

Test: open your homepage in an incognito tab. Count how many of Clay's removed phrases — like **"This spreadsheet fills itself"** — would help a first-time visitor understand what you do. If you find three or more missing category-explanation phrases, you may be targeting buyers who already know you while losing everyone else.
