## Clay's CTA and conversion path: from gated access to open trial

### Clay's primary CTAs drop the waitlist gate

Clay replaced **"Request access"** with **"Start free trial"** and **"Get a demo"**. The old CTA required approval before entry. The new ones let visitors decide their own path — self-serve trial or sales call — without asking permission first.

### Clay removed template CTAs: who that path served

The 2021 page offered **"Get template->"** and **"All templates"** as standalone CTAs. These let visitors see Clay applied to a specific workflow before signing up. The current page removed both. Visitors who want to browse a working example before committing to a trial no longer have that option above the fold.

### Test: does your CTA set match Clay's post-2021 shift?

Open your homepage. Count how many CTAs above the fold let someone explore without signing up. Clay went from two (**"Get template->"**, **"Explore documentation->"**) to zero. If you made the same shift, you are betting that your headline and feature descriptions alone will convince visitors to start a trial.
