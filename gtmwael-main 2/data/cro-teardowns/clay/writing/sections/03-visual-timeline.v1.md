## Clay's homepage positioning: how the headline changed from 2021 to 2026

### What changed

The original headline was just the product name: **"Clay"**. The current homepage opens with **"Go to market with unique data — and the ability to act on it"**.

The 2021 page also included a section heading that explained the product's core function: **"This spreadsheet fills itself."** That line no longer appears anywhere on the current page.

### Why it matters

The new headline assumes visitors already know what Clay is. It speaks to visitors who are comparing data enrichment tools, not visitors still learning what data enrichment means.

This is category positioning — the page now treats "go to market with unique data" as something the visitor already wants, rather than something Clay needs to explain or justify. The page stopped teaching the category and started claiming it.

### What it costs

If your homepage depends on non-branded search, referrals from general startup content, or paid ads targeting "spreadsheet automation" or similar generic terms, dropping the category explanation may reduce conversion. Visitors who arrive without knowing what problem you solve need more context, not less.

Test: open your homepage in an incognito tab. Can someone who has never heard of your company understand what it does in five seconds? If not, you may be targeting buyers who already know you — and losing everyone else.
