## Clay homepage audience shift: from practitioner tool to GTM platform

### Who the old page served

The 2021 page spoke to individual salespeople and marketers doing manual prospecting work. The meta description promised **"No more million tab opening and manual copy pasting"** — language for someone personally frustrated by repetitive tasks. The headline was just **"Clay"** — a brand-only H1 that provided no value proposition for first-time visitors.

### Who the new page serves

The 2026 page speaks to GTM leaders evaluating data infrastructure. The headline now reads **"Go to market with unique data — and the ability to act on it"** and the meta description highlights **"150+ premium data sources and AI research agents"**. These visitors are evaluating platform capabilities, not solving an immediate workflow pain.

### What this means for the sales process

The old page offered **"Get template->"** and **"Explore documentation->"** CTAs — paths for immediate self-serve exploration. The new page offers **"Get a demo"** and **"Read case study"** — paths for sales-assisted evaluation. Test: Open your homepage and count how many primary CTAs above the fold require zero commitment (sign up, explore, try) vs. scheduled contact (demo, call, enterprise). If all CTAs are demo-gated but you still rely on viral self-serve adoption, you may have a conversion gap.
