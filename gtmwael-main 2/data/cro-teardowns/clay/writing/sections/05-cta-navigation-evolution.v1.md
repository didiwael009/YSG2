## Clay's CTA shift: from access request to instant trial

### What changed

The primary CTA moved from **"Request access"** to **"Start building for free"**. Clay removed mid-funnel educational CTAs like **"Watch video"** and **"Explore documentation"** and added harder commitment options: **"Get a demo"** and **"Contact us"**. The path split into two directions — self-serve trial or talk-to-sales — and removed the in-between steps.

### Who this filters out

People who want to understand what Clay does before committing to anything. The old **"Watch video"** and **"Explore documentation"** buttons served visitors still deciding whether this type of tool fits their workflow at all. The new CTAs assume you already know you want Clay or a competitor — you are just deciding which one.

### Audit your own page

Open your homepage and count how many CTAs let someone learn more without starting a trial or booking a call. If that number is zero, you are filtering out early-stage traffic. Check your analytics: if most visitors arrive from non-branded search or cold ads, that filter may be costing you pipeline.
