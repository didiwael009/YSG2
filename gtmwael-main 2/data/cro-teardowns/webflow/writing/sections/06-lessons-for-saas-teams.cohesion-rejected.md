## Three homepage decisions from Webflow's repositioning that SaaS teams can test

### Primary CTA shift — Webflow elevated "Contact Sales" above "Get started — it's free"

Those business pressures translate into three decisions you can test directly. Webflow moved **"Contact Sales"** into the primary CTA position and demoted the free-tier entry point to secondary. This pattern is worth testing if your self-serve volume is healthy but average deal size has stalled — and only if your sales team already has a qualification process in place. Without that infrastructure, a primary sales CTA fills a pipeline no one can work. Visit your homepage and ask: which CTA does a mid-market buyer see first?

### Naming an emerging category — Webflow added "Explore AEO" as a standalone CTA

Webflow built a CTA around **"Explore AEO"** — Answer Engine Optimization, ranking in AI-generated results rather than traditional search — at a moment when the term is not yet standard. By naming the CTA after a phrase it is also promoting, Webflow positions itself to appear in conversations for a term it helped introduce. This is worth testing only if your product genuinely leads a behaviour the market has not yet named. Visit your homepage and check whether any CTA uses language your strongest competitors have already claimed.

### Removing founding-community signals — Webflow cut **"Who uses Webflow"** and **"Free until you're ready to launch"**

Both section headings spoke to cost-sensitive individual builders deciding whether to trust a new tool. Replacing them with **"300,000+ brands move the needle with Webflow"** and **"Make your website your competitive edge"** shifts the frame toward buyers justifying a purchase to a team or a CFO. That shift may leave early-stage freelancers and solo designers without a clear signal the product is for them — if those visitors no longer see themselves reflected on the page. Search your homepage text for "free", "easy", and "who uses" — then ask whether the visitors those words attract are still the buyers you want.
