## How Webflow demoted 'Free until you're ready to launch' to make room for 'Contact Sales' and 'Explore AEO'

### How Webflow's CTA stack changed — and what each version assumed about the visitor

The original single CTA — **"Get started — it's free"** — assumed the visitor needed reassurance before committing. Cost was a barrier worth removing upfront. The new stack leads with **"Contact Sales"** and **"Talk to sales"**, with **"Start for free"** appearing as a secondary option. That ordering suggests the page now expects a buyer who is already past the "is this affordable?" question.

Three action-specific CTAs also appeared: **"Start building"**, **"Start publishing"**, and **"Start optimizing"**. These point visitors toward distinct workflows rather than a single entry point. The page is no longer one door — it is several, sorted by what the visitor already intends to do.

**"Explore AEO"** is the most telling addition. AEO — Agent Experience Optimization, a category Webflow is actively naming — is not a term most visitors will arrive knowing. Placing a CTA around it before demand exists is a bet on creating that demand, not capturing it.

### What Webflow added to navigation — and what the removals signal about the visitor it expects

Eight navigation items were added; none were removed. But the CTA-removed list shows what disappeared from the broader page experience. Items like **"University"**, **"Courses"**, **"Ebooks"**, **"Templates"**, and **"Events"** — all education and community resources — were stripped out. So was the social proof anchor **"Showcase See the best sites #MadeInWebflow."**

That is a lot of onboarding infrastructure gone. It suggests the page no longer expects visitors who need to learn Webflow before deciding. It expects visitors who have already decided.

**Founder test:** Pull up your own homepage. Count how many CTAs exist to teach versus how many exist to convert. If teaching CTAs outnumber conversion CTAs, your page may still be doing sales-enablement work your brand should have already done.
