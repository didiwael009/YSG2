## Webflow homepage 2020–2026: from designer empowerment tool to enterprise growth platform

### 2020 — a page built for someone learning Webflow for the first time

The 2020 page was built for someone encountering Webflow for the first time. A left-aligned headline — **"Break the code barrier"** — spoke directly to a technical frustration. Section headings like **"Design"**, **"Build"**, and **"Launch"** walked visitors through the product step by step, paired with isometric product imagery on the right. The page explained what Webflow was and what it could do.

### The shift — "Break the code barrier" became "Make your website a growth engine"

The headline change is the clearest marker of what moved. **"Break the code barrier"** names what the product removes — a technical obstacle. **"Make your website a growth engine"** names what the product produces — a business result. At the same time, a new section anchored by **"300,000+ brands move the needle with Webflow"** replaced language aimed at someone evaluating whether to try the product at all.

### Webflow 2026 — enterprise positioning replaces product walkthrough

The current page looks substantially different from the 2020 version. The section headings **"Design"**, **"Build"**, and **"Launch"** are gone. In their place: **"Webflow is the agentic web marketing platform for high-performing brands"** and **"From idea to impact, faster."** The page title now reads **"The agentic web platform for modern businesses."**

Open the Webflow homepage and ask: does the headline — **"Make your website a growth engine"** — match the first question your own buyer types into Google, or does it match the answer Webflow wants to give? That gap is worth knowing.
