## How Webflow demoted 'Free until you're ready to launch' to make room for 'Contact Sales' and 'Explore AEO'

### How Webflow's homepage CTA stack changed between 2020 and 2026

Both **"Contact Sales"** and **"Start for free"** now appear on the homepage — alongside **"Talk to sales"** deeper in the page. The original homepage carried a single entry point: **"Get started — it's free"**. Adding a sales-contact option alongside a free-start option suggests the page is now built for at least two different visitors: one ready to buy, one still evaluating.

Then there is **"Explore AEO"** — Answer Engine Optimization, the practice of making content visible inside AI-generated search results. Webflow labelled a CTA after a term most visitors cannot yet define — a bet on shaping demand before it fully exists.

### How Webflow's navigation changes signal who the company no longer needs to convince

Five education and community items disappeared from the navigation: **"Events"**, **"Live Stream"**, **"University"**, **"Courses"**, and **"Ebooks"**. Meanwhile, eight items were added. The removed items served visitors still deciding whether Webflow was the right tool. Their removal signals the homepage may no longer be optimising for that undecided visitor — pointing instead toward someone who arrives already familiar with the platform.

The section heading **"Free until you're ready to launch"** was also removed from the page. That line was a direct reassurance to designers and freelancers with no upfront budget.

Its removal means that reassurance no longer exists anywhere visitors can see it. If those visitors still arrive in meaningful numbers, they no longer have an obvious on-ramp.

**Founder test:** Webflow removed five education nav items in this period. Count how many equivalent items your own navigation contains. If the number has dropped to zero, check whether an undecided visitor still has a learning path — or whether your nav now assumes everyone arrives ready to commit.
