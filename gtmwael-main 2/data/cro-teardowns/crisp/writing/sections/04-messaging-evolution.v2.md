## Crisp's messaging shift: from trial urgency to AI platform positioning

### Who Crisp's old homepage served

The original page spoke to founders choosing their first support tool and ready to try it immediately. The page title positioned Crisp as the **"#1 Business Messaging Platform For Startups & SMB's"**, and the meta description ended with **"14 Days free trial. No credit-card required. Try now !"** — language written for someone comparing options today.

### Who Crisp's new homepage serves

The current page addresses operations leaders at established companies who need multi-channel inboxes, integrations, and admin controls. The new title positions Crisp as **"The AI Customer Support Platform for Every Business"**, and the meta description now describes a platform **"leading businesses through the AI-driven revolution"** — vocabulary that assumes the reader already understands customer support platforms and is evaluating AI-specific capabilities.

### What this means for the sales process

The urgency cues are gone — no trial countdown, no exclamation marks — and the page now uses platform vocabulary that assumes the reader already understands the category. This suggests Crisp expects a longer evaluation process rather than immediate trial signups. Open your homepage in a private tab. Count how many words in the headline assume the visitor already knows your product category. If it's more than half, you may have moved past the trial-ready buyer.
