## Crisp homepage positioning: how the headline changed between 2022 and 2026

### What changed

The 2022 headline read **"Give your customer experience a human touch"** — a sentence that explained what Crisp did. The 2026 headline reads **"Augment your customer experience. AI made to support your team & customers."** The page no longer tells you what problem the product solves. It names a technology category and expects you already know why that matters.

### Why it matters

If a visitor arrives without prior familiarity with AI support platforms, the headline may leave them unclear on what Crisp does. The page now speaks to buyers who already believe AI customer support is a category worth evaluating — not to visitors still deciding whether they need support software at all. That is a narrower audience than the 2022 version served.

### What it costs

Remove your company name from your headline and ask: could a cold visitor name your product category? If not, your headline may filter out visitors who need the explanation. Test this by reading your headline as if you had never heard of your company. Does it tell you what the product is, or does it assume you already know?
