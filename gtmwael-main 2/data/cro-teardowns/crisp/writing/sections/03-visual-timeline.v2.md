## Crisp homepage positioning: how the headline changed between 2022 and 2026

### What changed

The 2022 headline promised **"Give your customer experience a human touch"** — a phrase about how support should feel. The 2026 headline now reads: **"Augment your customer experience. AI made to support your team & customers."** The page no longer describes the experience it creates. It names the tool it provides.

The meta description followed the same path. It once read "Business Messaging Platform for Startups & SMB's." It now reads "AI-powered multichannel messaging platform."

### Why it matters

The new headline assumes the visitor already accepts AI as part of customer support infrastructure. It does not argue for AI — it describes what kind of AI Crisp provides.

That works if the visitor arrives already looking for an AI support tool. If a visitor does not yet know they need AI in their support stack, the headline no longer explains why they might.

### What it costs

The page no longer explains what customer support automation *is* — only what Crisp's version *does*. A visitor who has not yet decided to adopt AI finds no reason to stay.

Test this on your own page: open the current Crisp homepage and count how many times "AI" appears before the page explains what customer support problem it solves. If the count is 3 or higher, the page assumes the visitor already decided to adopt AI.
