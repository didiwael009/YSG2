## Crisp homepage positioning: how the headline shifted from human to AI

### What changed

The 2022 headline read: **"Give your customer experience a human touch"**. The 2026 headline reads: **"Augment your customer experience. AI made to support your team & customers."**

The page replaced a human-centered promise with an AI-centered one. The category explanation remained — both versions tell you what Crisp does — but the frame inverted. The product shifted from "make it feel human" to "AI that supports."

### Why it matters

The new headline assumes visitors already accept AI-driven support as desirable. The old headline assumed the opposite: that automation felt cold, and visitors wanted assurance the product would preserve human connection.

This is a belief shift. The page no longer reassures skeptics. It appeals to buyers who already want AI in their support workflow — and are comparing platforms that offer it.

### What it costs

If a meaningful share of your visitors still associate AI support with poor customer experience, this framing may fail to land. The headline no longer acknowledges that concern.

Test it: ask five target customers to read your headline aloud, then finish this sentence: "This product will help me…" If they cannot finish it accurately, the headline is asking them to believe something they do not yet.
