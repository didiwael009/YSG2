## Crisp homepage positioning: how the headline changed from "human touch" to AI agent

### What changed

The 2022 headline read **"Give your customer experience a human touch"** — a statement that assumed the visitor needed convincing that customer service could be personal. By 2026, the headline reads **"Augment your customer experience. AI made to support your team & customers."** The shift is not just from "human" to "AI." It is from explaining what Crisp does to announcing what Crisp now represents: an AI-native support platform.

### Why it matters

The new headline no longer tells you what problem Crisp solves. It expects you already know. This is positioning shift — when a company stops explaining its category and starts claiming ownership of a new one. The visitor must now arrive believing that AI customer support is a real, mature category worth evaluating. If they land on the page still asking "what is this?", the headline will not answer that question.

### What it costs

Anyone arriving without prior belief in AI customer support will bounce before understanding what Crisp actually does. To test if your own homepage has this risk: remove your company name mentally and ask whether a cold visitor could name your product category from the headline alone. If not, you are trading explanation for aspiration — and that only pays off when your brand already does the explanation work.
