## Crisp homepage positioning: how the headline changed from human touch to AI augmentation

### What changed

The 2022 headline promised **"Give your customer experience a human touch"** — a product claim about style, not capability. The 2026 headline now reads: **"Augment your customer experience. AI made to support your team & customers."** The page stopped framing the product as a way to preserve something human, and started framing it as a way to scale what humans cannot do alone.

### Why it matters

The new headline assumes the visitor already accepts AI as part of customer support infrastructure. It does not argue for AI — it describes what kind of AI Crisp provides. That works if the visitor arrives already looking for an AI support tool. It may confuse visitors who are still comparing live chat platforms or considering whether to add automation at all. The page now speaks to buyers one decision deeper in the category.

### What it costs

The page no longer explains what customer support automation *is* — only what Crisp's version *does*. If a visitor does not yet believe AI belongs in their support stack, the headline offers no reason to stay. Test this on your own page: remove the word describing your category (e.g. "CRM", "help desk", "live chat") and see if a cold visitor can still tell what you sell. If not, you still need the explanation the page used to provide.
