## How Crisp's conversion path changed from 2022 to 2026

### What changed

Crisp replaced four generic CTAs with ten product-specific offers. The old homepage used **"Get Started"**, **"Try Crisp for free"**, and **"Request a demo"** — broad labels that work for any visitor. The new version splits the path: **"Get your AI Agent"**, **"Start Free Trial"**, **"Book a demo"**, **"Download Crisp for macOS"**. Each button now names a specific product feature or commitment level.

### Who this filters out

The new CTAs assume you already know which part of Crisp you want. Someone comparing live chat tools for the first time now faces a choice between AI Agent, Knowledge Base, and messaging channels before understanding what Crisp does. The page no longer serves people who need to explore the full platform before choosing an entry point.

### Audit your own page

Count how many CTAs appear above the fold. If your visitor needs to choose between product modules or feature sets before clicking, check whether non-branded traffic can make that choice. Run a five-second test: show the hero section to someone unfamiliar with your product and ask them which button they would click.
