## Crisp's messaging shift: from startups to every business

### Who the old page served

The original page spoke to founders and small teams choosing their first support tool. The page title called Crisp the **"#1 Business Messaging Platform For Startups & SMB's"**, and the meta description emphasized **"14 Days free trial. No credit-card required. Try now !"** — language written for someone comparing options and ready to test immediately.

### Who the new page serves

The current page addresses operations leaders at established companies who need platform-level tools. The new title positions Crisp as **"The AI Customer Support Platform for Every Business"**, and the meta description now describes a company **"leading businesses through the AI-driven revolution"** — vocabulary that assumes the reader already understands customer support platforms and is evaluating AI-specific capabilities.

### What this means for the sales process

The new page appears built for a longer buying cycle that includes demos, internal approvals, and integration planning. The urgency cues are gone. The exclamation marks are gone. The page now reads like vendor documentation rather than trial activation copy. Does your homepage still speak to the buyer who wants to start today?
