## Crisp's messaging shift: from startups to AI-powered support buyers

### Who the old page served

The 2022 page spoke to early-stage founders choosing their first customer communication tool. The meta description called out **"Startups & SMB's"** and emphasized **"14 Days free trial. No credit-card required. Try now!"** The exclamation marks and trial urgency suggest a buyer who needs to be convinced to start, not one already comparing vendors.

### Who the new page serves

The current page speaks to buyers who already know they need a customer support platform and are now evaluating AI capabilities. The headline now reads **"Augment your customer experience. AI made to support your team & customers."** The meta description positions Crisp as **"the ultimate all-in-one AI-powered multichannel messaging platform"** — language for someone comparing feature sets, not discovering the category.

### What this means for the sales process

The page now expects visitors who are further along in their buying process. The urgency language is gone. The focus shifted to platform capabilities and AI differentiation. This suggests Crisp may now rely more on demos or sales conversations than instant self-serve signups. Does your homepage assume the visitor is ready to buy, or still learning what the product does?
