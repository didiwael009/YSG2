## How Foreplay replaced 'Hire Us' and 'View Project' with a four-product nav and a free trial funnel

### Foreplay's CTA changes: from client transactions to free trial signup

The original CTAs assumed a visitor ready to hire someone. **"Hire Us"**, **"Continue to Checkout"**, **"Our Work"**, **"More Work"**, and named client campaigns — **"Strike Launching a Pre-Workout"**, **"OwnersBox Drew Brees' Fantasy Hotline"**, **"Healthy Crunch Sling'n Snacks"**, **"AdTrax Premium Music for your Ads"**, and **"AdFlow Video Ads for your Brand"** — all pointed toward a services transaction. The new CTAs assume a visitor ready to try the product themselves: **"Start Free Trial"**, **"Get Free Trial"**, and **"Start For Free"** replace every one of them.

Four added CTAs describe specific jobs: **"Save & Organize"**, **"Automate Transcription"**, **"Easily Share & Collaborate"**, and **"24/7 Ad Library Scraper"**. Each targets a different task, which suggests the page is now built for more than one type of user.

If named client campaigns were the page's primary proof for first-time visitors, removing them before a replacement trust signal is in place may leave a gap that free-trial messaging alone does not yet fill.

### Foreplay's navigation changes: eight items added, four removed

Four navigation items were removed and eight were added. The removed items are not individually named in the evidence, so their content cannot be characterised. What the pattern does show is a net expansion — from a lighter nav to one that needs to orient visitors across multiple areas of the product.

The eight items added are not listed individually in the evidence either, but the total count signals the company now expects visitors to need more orientation, not less.

**Founder test:** Foreplay removed four unnamed nav items and replaced them with job-specific CTAs like **"Save & Organize"** and **"Automate Transcription"**. Count how many of your own nav items describe what you do *for* a client versus what a visitor can do *themselves* right now. Any label that implies a handoff may be agency framing in disguise.
