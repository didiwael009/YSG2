## Foreplay homepage 2021–2026: from black-hero agency statement to enterprise workflow platform

That headline swap played out over five years of layout changes.

### 2021 — a startup speaking to creatives, not buyers

The original Foreplay homepage made no attempt to explain a product category. The above-fold layout was a centered hero: bold white sans-serif type on a black background, nothing else. The headline read **"WE PRACTICE DOPE DESIGN & SELL SH\*T ONLINE"**. Navigation offered **"Work"**, **"Services"**, and **"Play Haus"**. The page title confirmed the framing: **"Foreplay | D2C Creative Advertising Agency"**. This was a personality-led brand statement, not a software product page.

### Mid-2022 — the visual inflection point

The shift from agency identity to software product happened visibly around mid-2022. The layout change between the 2021 and 2022 snapshots was one of the largest in the dataset — a near-complete redesign rather than an incremental update. The hero moved to a dark full-bleed layout with a serif headline and abstract product imagery. The tone shifted from identity statement to outcome claim.

### 2024–2026 — enterprise polish replaces startup energy

By mid-2024, the page carried a multi-image product carousel alongside **"The Complete Winning Ad Workflow"** — and proof density had risen from none to medium. By January 2026, the background had flipped from dark to light, product screenshots were integrated directly into the hero, and the evidence describes the design as enterprise-grade, earning a visual sophistication score of 5 out of 5. The meta description now reads: **"From advertising inspiration to creative analytics."** Agency personality is gone entirely.

**Test this on your own site:** visit your homepage and read only the headline and navigation labels. Could a visitor tell whether you sell a service or a software product? If the answer is unclear, your page may still be in agency-statement mode.
