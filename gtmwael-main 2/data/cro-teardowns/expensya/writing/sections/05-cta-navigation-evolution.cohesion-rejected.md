## How Expensya changed its homepage CTAs and navigation to target the French-speaking buyer

### Expensya removed four browsing-stage CTAs and replaced them with one demo request

If the copy now names a specific buyer, the buttons followed. Four CTAs disappeared: **"Free trial"**, **"Discover our solutions"**, **"Find out more"**, and **"Discover all integrations"**. Each assumed a visitor still deciding whether to engage. The replacement — **"Réservez une démo"** — assumes the opposite. If traffic quality held steady during this period, the shift suggests the page is now built for visitors who arrive informed and ready to talk to someone, not visitors who need room to explore first.

### Expensya's homepage language pivot signals a change in who the page is written for

The most specific signal in the evidence is not the navigation count — it is the language. Seven English-language section headings were removed. Fourteen French-language headings were added. The old page opened with **"Equip yourself with the most comprehensive business spend and expense management solution"**. The new page asks **"Comment gérez-vous vos notes de frais?"** — "How do you manage your expense reports?" — and closes with **"Prêt à découvrir une meilleure façon de gérer les dépenses professionnelles?"** That is a direct address to a French-speaking buyer, not a translation of the original.

At the same time, 8 navigation items were removed and 8 were added. The specific labels are not listed in the evidence, but if the navigation shifted to match the French-language page, visitors who arrived via English-language search may find fewer familiar entry points — which, if that traffic still exists, may push more qualifying work onto the sales team.

**Founder test:** Check whether the primary language of your homepage headings and CTAs matches the language your highest-intent visitors actually arrive in. Expensya switched its entire page from English to French — not just the CTA. If your page language and your best traffic are misaligned, that gap is worth closing before you change anything else.
