## What SaaS teams can study from Expensya's market-retreat homepage strategy

### Language as market commitment — Expensya switched its entire homepage to French

With those forces in view, the moves become a checklist you can run. Open your analytics now: check what language your highest-converting visitors use. If one language dominates, your homepage may still be hedging against a market you have already won. Expensya replaced **"Automated spend management for all companies"** with **"Voici à quoi devrait ressembler la gestion des dépenses"** — and kept **"Anglais"** as a navigation toggle rather than deleting English access entirely. This move makes most sense if your top organic traffic cohort already comes overwhelmingly from one country and one language.

### Compliance framing as a buyer-role signal — Expensya added **"Politiques de dépenses"** and **"Améliorez la conformité"**

Read your homepage's first three section headings right now. If they describe what the product does rather than what a finance leader is accountable for, your page may be speaking to the wrong person in the buying team. Expensya replaced **"Expensya fully automates your business expenditure"** with **"Fini le casse-tête administratif, place à la conformité"** — language consistent with targeting finance decision-makers who manage regulatory risk, not ops teams chasing efficiency.

### CTA shift from self-serve to sales-assisted — Expensya removed **"Free trial"** and added **"Réservez une démo"**

Count how many of your current CTAs require zero sales interaction. Expensya dropped **"Free trial"** and **"Discover our solutions"** entirely, replacing them with **"Réservez une démo"** and **"Contacter un commercial"**. That move signals a shift from self-serve toward an assisted sales path — consistent with targeting buyers who need a guided evaluation rather than a trial signup. This pattern is worth testing if your sales data shows free trials generating volume but not the deal sizes or customer profiles you want to grow. If your sales team does not yet have capacity to convert demo requests at scale, removing a free-trial CTA before that motion is in place may collapse top-of-funnel volume without an offsetting gain.
