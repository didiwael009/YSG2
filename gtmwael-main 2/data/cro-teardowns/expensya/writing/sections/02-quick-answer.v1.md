Expensya's biggest homepage change was its primary headline, which shifted from **"Automated spend management for all companies"** to **"Voici à quoi devrait ressembler la gestion des dépenses."** The page now speaks to French-speaking finance teams who already know what expense management software is — and who need to be convinced of a better standard, not educated on the basics. If your homepage still depends on cold, English-language search traffic to fill a self-serve trial funnel, copying this move may collapse your top-of-funnel before your sales team can compensate.
