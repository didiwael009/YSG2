## Expensya homepage 2020–2026: messaging and visual redesign across six years

### October 2020 — a homepage built for any company, anywhere

The 2020 homepage was built for scale, not selection: the headline and navigation both assumed any company could be the customer. The headline read **"Automated spend management for all companies."** Navigation labels reinforced this — **"Small," "Mid," "Large"** — covering every company size rather than addressing one kind of buyer.

The layout matched that openness: a centered hero, isometric product illustration on the right, and a green CTA below. The brand maturity was labelled "developing."

### Expensya messaging shift: English out, French in

The most visible change is the headline. It moved from English to French: **"Voici à quoi devrait ressembler la gestion des dépenses."** That is not a direct translation of the original — if intentional, it signals a shift toward a French-speaking audience rather than a universal one, though it may equally reflect a regional SEO or localisation decision.

The meta description shifted too: from an English process explanation to a French statement positioning Expensya as **"le logiciel intelligent de gestion des dépenses pour les équipes financières modernes."** Three CTAs disappeared in the same period: **"Free trial," "Discover our solutions,"** and **"Find out more."**

### June 2026 — a polished page that may address a more specific reader

The current page uses a full-bleed layout with a female portrait photograph and a French text overlay. The design era moved from "growth" to "mature," and the brand maturity label moved from "developing" to "polished."

The page now addresses a more specific reader than in 2020 — if this language change is intentional, a non-French visitor may find fewer obvious entry points than before.

Open Expensya's current homepage, look at the headline and the three removed CTAs — **"Free trial," "Discover our solutions," "Find out more"** — and ask: can a first-time visitor who does not read French still find a next step, or has that path been closed?
