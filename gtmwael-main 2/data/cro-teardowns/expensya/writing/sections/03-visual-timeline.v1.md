## Expensya homepage 2020–2025: from isometric automation showcase to human-first French identity

### October 2020 — a product explanation built for anyone, anywhere

The 2020 homepage opened with a centered-hero layout: large headline, isometric product illustration on the right, and a green CTA button below. The headline was direct and universal: **"Automated spend management for all companies."**

Navigation reinforced the same logic. Labels read **"Small," "Mid," "Large"** — sizing the product for any company, not a specific kind of buyer. The design era was "growth," the brand maturity labelled "developing."

### The visible shift — English out, French in

The most visible change is the headline. It moved from English to French: **"Voici à quoi devrait ressembler la gestion des dépenses."** That is not a translation. It is a different audience entirely.

The meta description shifted too — from an English process explanation to a French statement positioning Expensya as **"le logiciel intelligent de gestion des dépenses pour les équipes financières modernes."**

Three CTAs were removed: **"Free trial," "Discover our solutions,"** and **"Find out more."**

### June 2026 — polished brand, narrower stage

The current page uses a full-bleed layout with a female portrait photograph and French text overlay. The brand maturity label moved from "developing" to "polished." The primary message type moved from "outcome" to "story."

More polish arrived alongside a smaller target audience, not a larger one.

Visit your homepage and read your headline aloud. Does it assume the reader already knows what your product does — or does it explain it to anyone who might land there?
